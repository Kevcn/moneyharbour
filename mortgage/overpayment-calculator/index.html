What this calculation shows

We amortise the current balance at the entered rate and term, then run the same schedule after applying the lump sum and monthly overpayment. The difference is the estimated time and interest saved.

Before making an overpayment

Keep an emergency buffer, compare the mortgage rate with what cash could earn after tax, and confirm whether your lender reduces the term, payment, or both. Overpaying is usually irreversible.
