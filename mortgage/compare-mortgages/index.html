True cost is not simply payments plus fees. We also compare the balance remaining, so a deal does not appear cheaper merely because it repays less capital. Rates are assumed unchanged during the comparison period.

How true cost is calculated

For each deal: payments made + upfront fees − cashback + balance remaining − original mortgage. This captures interest, fees and the effect of adding a fee to the mortgage. It excludes early repayment charges after the selected period and any different revert rate.
