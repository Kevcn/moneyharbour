About Money Harbour

About Money Harbour

Money Harbour is a small independent website building free, practical tools for UK money decisions. It does not handle your money, sell financial products or provide regulated advice.

Why it exists

Many financial pages either bury the useful answer under jargon or make the result conditional on creating an account, entering contact details or speaking to a salesperson. Money Harbour takes the opposite approach: give people the useful tool first, explain the assumptions and make any commercial relationship visible.

The operating principle: useful before impressive, transparent before persuasive.

What “independent” means here

Money Harbour is not owned by a bank, lender, broker, accounting-software company or comparison network. Calculator outputs are determined by the numbers entered and the published method—not by commercial partners.

The site may eventually use advertising or affiliate links to fund its operation. When it does, those relationships will be labelled and explained. See how Money Harbour makes money.

What it is—and is not

  • A collection of free UK calculators, generators and explanatory guides.
  • General information designed to help people understand a decision.
  • Not a bank, lender, broker, accountant or financial adviser.
  • Not a substitute for regulated financial, tax or legal advice.

Corrections and feedback

If you spot an outdated figure, unclear assumption or technical problem, please send a correction. Changing information is dated so that readers can judge whether it is still current.
