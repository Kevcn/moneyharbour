Privacy

Privacy

Money Harbour is built to collect as little personal information as practical. Most tools work locally in the browser without sending the entered data to us.

Calculator and invoice data

Salary, bonus, tax code, pension and student-loan choices entered into the salary calculator stay in the browser. Numbers entered into the remortgage calculator also stay in the browser. Business, client, invoice and payment details entered into the invoice generator stay in the browser. Money Harbour does not receive or store those entries.

Aggregate analytics

The site uses GoatCounter to understand aggregate page visits and interactions such as calculator use, template downloads and print actions. It is used as privacy-friendly analytics and does not set personal tracking cookies on this site.

Email and contact forms

If you deliberately submit an email signup or contact form, the submitted information is sent through FormSubmit so that Money Harbour can receive it by email. Do not include bank details, passwords, National Insurance numbers or other sensitive financial information in a message.

Messages may be retained for correspondence, corrections and site administration. They are not sold.

External links

External websites have their own privacy practices. If advertising or affiliate partners are added, their links and the commercial relationship will be disclosed, but their sites will be governed by their own policies.

Cookies

Money Harbour does not currently use advertising cookies or personalised tracking cookies. This policy will be updated before introducing technology that materially changes that position.
