Commercial transparency

How Money Harbour Makes Money

Money Harbour is designed to be funded eventually by advertising and carefully selected affiliate links—not by charging people to use its calculators.

Current status: Money Harbour is an early-stage test site. At the time this page was published, it did not display paid advertisements or active affiliate links.

Potential future revenue

The site may earn money in two ways:

  1. Advertising: payment for displaying clearly distinguishable advertisements alongside free content and tools.
  2. Affiliate links: a commission when a visitor follows a labelled partner link and completes a qualifying action.

What will not change

  • Calculators remain free and usable without an account.
  • A partner cannot pay to change a calculator result.
  • Affiliate links will be labelled near the link—not only in a distant footer.
  • Sponsored placement will be distinguished from independent explanation.
  • Compensation will not be presented as proof that a provider is “best”.

How commercial links will appear

Example disclosure: “Money Harbour may earn a commission if you use this link. It does not affect your calculation.”

This keeps the neutral result separate from the optional commercial next step. When partners are introduced, this page will be updated to explain the arrangements in force.

No personalised recommendation

An affiliate link is not a personalised recommendation or regulated financial advice. Visitors should compare eligibility, fees, terms and alternatives before choosing a provider.
